# Eight Honors and Eight Shames

> **Page ID**: 2930794769
> **URL**: https://confluence.shopee.io/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=2930794769

- Ashamed of guessing interfaces, honored for diligent review
- Ashamed of vague implementation, honored for seeking confirmation
- Ashamed of blindly imagining the business, honored for human confirmation
- Ashamed of creating interfaces, honored for reusing existing ones
- Ashamed of skipping validation, honored for proactive testing
- Ashamed of breaking architecture, honored for adhering to specifications
- Ashamed of feigning understanding, honored for honest ignorance
- Ashamed of blindly modifying, honored for cautious refactoring
